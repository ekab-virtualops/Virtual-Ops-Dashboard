CS Complaint – Transaction Failed Analysis

May – July 2026

Executive Summary

Objective

• Monitor whether the share of "Transaction Failed" complaints is increasing, identify the main business root causes behind user complaints, and determine the highest-impact improvement actions.

Data Finding

• Although total CS complaints decreased by 60.8%, Transaction Failed complaint share increased from 4.7% to 21.3%. The majority of complaints (89.9%) are related to Refund Visibility and understanding why transactions failed, indicating the primary issue is refund communication rather than actual supplier or system failures (5.3%).

Next Plan

• Prioritize improvements on Refund Visibility and transaction failure messaging, continue monitoring high-complaint categories (Paket Data, Token Listrik) and suppliers, and track the impact on Transaction Failed complaints over the coming months.

Key Findings

  • • Paket Data was the largest Transaction Failed category in July with 101 complaints (44.3% of TF).
  • • The dominant business root cause in July was Refund Visibility, representing 60.1% of Transaction Failed complaints.
  • • Refund Visibility had the biggest increase from May to July (+83 complaints).
  • • Only 5.6% of Transaction Failed complaints were caused by actual supplier or system failures. The majority (55.2%) were related to refund understanding and communication.
  • • Transaction Failed increased by 99 complaints from May to July, while overall complaints declined 60.8%.
